## 1. Executive Statement & Scholarly Purpose

This open-source repository and its accompanying web reader are created strictly as a **non-commercial, educational, and academic digital humanities initiative**. 

The sole objective of this project is to assist contemporary college students, philosophers, and literature readers in comprehending the dense, dialectical argumentation of Albert Camus’s 1942 essay *Le Mythe de Sisyphe* by providing:
1. A **sentence-by-sentence modern literary parallel translation** that decodes mid-20th-century academic syntax without dumbing down philosophical concepts.
2. Original scholarly commentary titled **"The Philosophical Move"** for every argument block, elucidating Camus's epistemological and ethical pivots.
3. An open-source interactive comparative reading interface.

**This project does not seek, nor does it accept, any monetary compensation, commercial sponsorship, advertising revenue, licensing fees, or financial donations of any kind.**

## 3. Fair Use & Fair Dealing Statutory Declarations

The inclusion of historic excerpts from Justin O'Brien’s 1955 English translation within this repository is made in good faith pursuant to recognized international doctrines of **Fair Use** and **Fair Dealing** for educational study, criticism, commentary, and research.

### A. United States Law: 17 U.S. Code § 107 (Fair Use)
Under Section 107 of the U.S. Copyright Act of 1976, reproduction of copyrighted material for purposes such as criticism, comment, scholarship, or research is not an infringement of copyright. In analyzing the four statutory factors:
1. **Purpose and Character of the Use:** Highly transformative. The historic text is paired with modern parallel translations and extensive analytical keys ("The Philosophical Move") to facilitate close textual and philosophical analysis. The use is strictly nonprofit and educational.
2. **Nature of the Copyrighted Work:** A published philosophical essay originally issued in 1942 (French) and 1955 (English).
3. **Amount and Substantiality:** While comprehensive across the essay's core sections, the text is subdivided into discrete analytical units specifically arranged to facilitate line-by-line comparative critique.
4. **Effect on the Potential Market:** Non-substitutive. Because this is formatted as a comparative academic study tool and explicitly directs readers to purchase the commercial paperback, it does not harm the market for the commercial trade edition, and may in fact stimulate demand for the official text among students who previously found it inaccessible.

### B. Singapore Law: Singapore Copyright Act 2021 (Division 2, Sections 190–194)
Under Singapore's Copyright Act 2021, statutory exceptions explicitly protect fair dealing for:
- **Research and Study** (Sections 190–192)
- **Criticism and Review** (Sections 193–194)

The comparative presentation, philosophical analysis, and critical annotations in this project fall directly under fair dealing for educational research and critical review.

### C. United Kingdom & Commonwealth Law
Under the *Copyright, Designs and Patents Act 1988* (CDPA):
- **Section 29:** Fair dealing with a work for the purposes of non-commercial research or private study.
- **Section 30:** Fair dealing for the purposes of criticism, review, or quotation, provided that it is accompanied by sufficient acknowledgement.

### D. International Law: Berne Convention
Under Article 10(2) of the *Berne Convention for the Protection of Literary and Artistic Works*, national legislation may permit the utilization of literary or artistic works by way of illustration in publications, broadcasts, or sound/visual recordings for teaching, provided such utilization is compatible with fair practice.
